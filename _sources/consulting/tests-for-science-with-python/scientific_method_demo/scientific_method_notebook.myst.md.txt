#### Mixtures, Mixing, and Entropy
If evenly mixed, eventually the ph in a mixture of substances with different Ph will be roughly near the average ph of the scaled masses of substances; the ph will be in the middle of the ph of the things mixed.

If we were to sample the ph at various points in a solution over time and plot the scores,
there would be a distribution with a central tendency.

There could be exothermic (yields heat) and/or endothermic (consumes heat) reactions from mixing things with different ph.

Eventually, given mixing due to physical effects over time like:
 convection, titration (vibration), density (mass/volume), and buoyancy
the mixture will be uniformly distributed;
such that it doesn't matter at which vector (point) in the solution a sample is drawn from;
we'll get the same score wherever we measure it, after a given amount of time.
